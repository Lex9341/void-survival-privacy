VOID SURVIVOR — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

This policy describes how the Android game VOID SURVIVOR (package com.voidsurvivor.game) handles your information.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device data. It has no network access (no INTERNET permission), and contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting, accounts or third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Game progress and settings (currency, upgrades, skins, records, language, volume) are stored only locally on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data, and can be reset from the in-game settings. If you have Android device backup enabled, Android may include this local game data in your personal Google backup; that backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

Permissions

Vibrate — used only for in-game haptic feedback; it can be turned off in the game settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if online features or purchases are added), the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Политика конфиденциальности (RU)

VOID SURVIVOR не собирает, не передаёт и не продаёт никакие персональные данные. У приложения нет доступа к сети, нет рекламы, аналитики и аккаунтов. Прогресс хранится только на вашем устройстве и удаляется вместе с приложением. Разрешение «Вибрация» используется только для тактильной отдачи в игре и отключается в настройках.
